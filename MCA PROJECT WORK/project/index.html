Abstract

Road accidents are a leading cause of death and injury worldwide, often occurring repeatedly in specific high-risk zones known as Black Spots. This project aims to develop an intelligent Black Spot Prediction System that uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Global Positioning System (GPS) technologies to enhance road safety by identifying and predicting accident-prone areas in real time.

The system collects historical accident data, analyzes geographical patterns, and applies machine learning algorithms to detect high-risk locations. When a driver enters or approaches a potential Black Spot, the system issues live safety alerts and suggests alternate, safer routes. The integration of real-time GPS tracking enables continuous monitoring of the user's location.

This project uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) to analyze past accident data and predict high-risk zones. The system studies road conditions, traffic flow, accident history, and weather factors to identify why accidents happen in specific areas. Once a Black Zone is detected, the system provides real-time alerts to drivers and suggests safer alternate routes.

Help & User Guide

Welcome to the Black Spot Prediction System. This guide will help you understand how to use the system step by step.

Step 1: Start by registering or logging in using your email, password, and mobile number with OTP verification.

Step 2: After successful login, click the “Proceed” button to launch the interactive map interface.

Step 3: In the map page, enter your starting location (Arrival) and destination.

Step 4: Click on “Show Route & Black Spots”. The system will display your route with accident-prone zones (Black Spots) marked on the map.

Step 5: Click on any black spot marker to see detailed information such as:

  • Risk alert (e.g., School Zone, Sharp Curve)
  • GPS coordinates
  • Safety tips (e.g., Drive slow, Use low beam)
  • 'Show Reason' button to know why it's a black spot
